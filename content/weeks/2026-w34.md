### The week in review: five retailers, two different results

Five large retailers reported second-quarter results between Tuesday and Thursday. The ones selling
everyday goods reported stronger growth in their existing stores than the ones selling home
improvement.

Target's comparable sales grew 3.8 percent and Ross Stores' comparable store sales grew 10%, both
driven mainly by customer traffic. Home Depot's comparable sales rose 1.7% and Lowe's rose 0.2%.
Walmart US comparable sales rose 2.6%.

**The pattern:** comparable sales was the figure that separated the group. Total sales grew at all five
companies. Looking only at total sales, the week would have read as five companies doing roughly the
same thing.

**Concept:** discretionary vs. staple spending

Some purchases happen on a schedule regardless of circumstances, like groceries and household basics.
Others can wait, like replacing a kitchen. Companies tend to get sorted into those two groups when
household budgets tighten.

**Why it matters:** this is one week of results from five companies, which is a small sample and one
that says nothing on its own about what comes next. It does show why the same headline, "sales grew,"
can describe two fairly different quarters.
