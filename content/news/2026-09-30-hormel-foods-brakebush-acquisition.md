### Hormel Foods agreed to buy a chicken company

About $1.055 billion. That's the price Hormel Foods, whose brands include SPAM, Skippy and Planters, agreed to pay for Brakebush, a chicken company based in Westfield, Wisconsin.

Brakebush brought in about $1.2 billion in net sales over its last 12 months. Hormel expects the deal to close in the first quarter of its 2027 fiscal year.

**Concept:** buying growth vs. building it

A company can grow by selling more through the business it already has, or by buying another company that already has customers and factories. Building usually takes years. Buying adds a whole company's sales once the deal closes, and the price gets paid up front.

**Why it matters:** When a company reports bigger sales a year from now, how much came from the business it already had, and how much did it buy?
