### Madison Square Garden Sports split the New York Rangers into their own company

On October 26, Madison Square Garden Sports handed its stockholders shares of a new company that holds the New York Rangers business. The parent is now called MSG Knickerbockers Corp. The Rangers company trades on the New York Stock Exchange.

The combined company brought in $1,153.8 million in revenue in the fiscal year that ended June 30, 2026. The filing puts $337.3 million of that with the Rangers side.

**Concept:** segment mix

One company can hold several businesses that grow at different speeds, and a single total hides which piece did what. A spin-off pulls one of those businesses out and gives it its own shares and its own reports. After the split, you can see each one on its own numbers.

**Why it matters:** When a team, brand or app you follow ends up under a different company name, worth asking: which business was doing the earning before the split?
