### Delta charged more per mile as its fuel bill climbed

60%. That's how much more Delta paid per gallon of fuel in its September quarter than a year earlier, using the company's adjusted fuel price.

So it charged more. Delta's passenger mile yield, the money it collects for each mile a passenger flies, rose 14%. Its chief commercial officer said revenue grew about 16% on flat capacity, the airline term for how many seats it flew and how far.

**Concept:** pricing power

Pricing power is whether a company can charge more and keep its customers. When a big cost goes up, a company that has it can pass part of the increase on to you. A company that lacks it has to absorb the cost, or risk you spending your money somewhere else.

**Why it matters:** Next time a fare looks higher than last year, is the airline covering a cost, or finding out how much you'll still pay?
