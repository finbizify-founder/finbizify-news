### Coca-Cola raised prices and still sold more drinks

Coca-Cola's net revenues reached $13.4 billion in its third quarter. The number of drinks it sold, counted in cases of 24 eight-ounce servings, grew 5%.

Price and mix added 1%. The company says that came mainly from pricing actions, partly offset by what it calls unfavorable mix.

**Concept:** pricing power

Pricing power is whether a company can raise prices without losing customers. The test is what happens to volume after the price goes up. If people keep buying as much or more, the brand has room to charge. If they drift to something else, that room is smaller than the company hoped.

**Why it matters:** Next time a drink you like costs more than it used to, notice whether you bought it anyway. How many price bumps would it take before you'd switch?
