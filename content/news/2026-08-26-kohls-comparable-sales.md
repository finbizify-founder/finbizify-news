### Kohl's total sales and its existing-store sales both slipped by the same amount

Kohl's reported second-quarter net sales of $3.3 billion, down 0.9% from a year earlier. Comparable
sales, which counts only stores open at least a year, were also down 0.9%.

**Concept:** comparable sales

When total sales and comparable sales move by close to the same amount, in either direction, it means
new and closed stores had little effect on the result. The existing business is carrying almost the
entire number.

**Why it matters:** the day before, Dick's Sporting Goods reported its own comparable stores growing.
Worth asking of any retailer: is this figure moving because of the stores it already had, or because
of stores it opened or closed?
