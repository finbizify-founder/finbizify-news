### Lennar used discounts to keep its homes selling

$372,000. That was the average price of a home Lennar delivered in its third quarter, down from $383,000 a year earlier.

Lennar, which calls itself one of the nation's leading homebuilders, said that price reflected about 12.0% in incentives. Plus what it called "base price adjustments necessary to sustain volume."

**Concept:** pricing power

Pricing power is whether a company can raise prices without losing customers. It runs in reverse too. When buyers can't stretch any further, a seller either cuts the price or watches sales slow down. An incentive is a discount by another name, so the sticker price can hold while the price you actually pay drops.

**Why it matters:** Next time you see a big "limited-time offer," ask whether the price went down or the seller found a quieter way to cut it.
