### Ross Stores' earnings included a tariff refund

Ross Stores reported second-quarter total sales up 13% and comparable store sales up 10%, primarily
driven by customer traffic. Operating profit included approximately $253 million from IEEPA tariff
refunds, which benefited earnings per share by about $0.60 of the $2.66 reported.

**Concept:** one-time items vs. ongoing business

Earnings can include money that came from running the business and money that came from a separate
event, like a refund, a legal settlement, or selling a building. Companies report the figures
separately so both can be seen.

**Why it matters:** separating the regular business from the one-off events is part of reading any
earnings report.
