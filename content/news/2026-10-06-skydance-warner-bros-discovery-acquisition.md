### Paramount finished buying Warner Bros. Discovery and took the name Skydance

On October 6, Paramount completed its purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery. The combined company is called Skydance.

It now owns HBO Max and Paramount+, plus CBS, CNN, Nickelodeon and the Warner Bros. studio. The company says it has 200+ million streaming subscribers across platforms, and that its streaming products will merge into a single service over time.

**Concept:** buying growth vs. building it

A company can grow by building: making more shows, signing up subscribers one at a time. Or it can buy another company and get its customers, shows and brands all at once. Buying is faster. It also means paying for everything up front and then fitting two companies together, which tends to be the hard part.

**Why it matters:** If your household pays for both HBO Max and Paramount+, here's a question to keep asking: once they become one app, does it cost more or less than the two did?
