### Jersey Mike's sales grew from new shops and more visits to existing ones

10%. That's how much sales across all Jersey Mike's stores grew in the second quarter compared with a year earlier.

Stores in the same-store base grew 2.3%, mostly from more transactions. And the chain opened 83 new stores in the quarter.

**Concept:** comparable sales

Total sales counts every shop, including the ones that just opened. Comparable sales, which Jersey Mike's calls same-store sales, counts only shops that have been open a while. Jersey Mike's says its total grows from both: new shops opening and existing ones selling more. When the total runs well ahead of the same-store number, new locations are doing a lot of the lifting.

**Why it matters:** Next time a chain seems to be on every corner, ask whether the shop near you is getting busier or there are just more shops.
