### Lowe's bought two companies during the quarter

Lowe's reported total sales for the quarter of $26.0 billion while comparable sales rose 0.2%. The
company recognized $96 million in pre-tax expenses associated with the acquisitions of Foundation
Building Materials and Artisan Design Group.

**Concept:** buying growth vs. building it

A company can grow by selling more through the business it already runs, or by purchasing another
business and adding its revenue. Both show up in total sales. Only the first shows up in comparable
sales.

**Why it matters:** Home Depot reported the day before with a similar shape, total sales growing faster
than existing stores. Worth asking of any company that reports growth: which of the two kinds was it?
