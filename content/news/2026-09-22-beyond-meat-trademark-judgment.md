### A judge cut one part of Beyond Meat's trademark verdict and kept the other

$37,500. That's what a federal judge reduced a $23.5 million jury award to on September 18, in a trademark case against Beyond Meat.

A jury had found Beyond Meat liable over taglines like "Plant-Based Great Taste." The trademark belongs to Vegadelphia Foods: "Where Great Taste Is Plant-Based."

The judge kept the other part of the award, $15.4 million of Beyond Meat's profits that it now owes the other side. Either side may still appeal.

**Concept:** one-time items vs. ongoing business

A court judgment comes from one event, separate from the everyday work of making and selling food. It can pull down a company's results for a period without telling you much about whether its products are selling. So when you read a company's numbers, it helps to set events like this aside and look at the regular business on its own.

**Why it matters:** Next time you're in the plant-based aisle, count how many brands use some mix of the same few words on the package.
