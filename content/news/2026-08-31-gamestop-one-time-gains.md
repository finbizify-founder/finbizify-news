### GameStop's store sales fell and its reported profit rose in the same quarter

GameStop reported preliminary second-quarter net sales expected between $780 million and $800
million, down from $972.2 million a year earlier. In the same release, the company expects net
income between $290 million and $310 million, up from $168.6 million a year earlier, which it said
includes about $238 million in net gains tied to its eBay stock and related investment.

**Concept:** one-time items vs. ongoing business

A company's reported profit can include gains or losses from things it owns that have nothing to do
with what it sells. GameStop holds shares of eBay, and the value of that holding moved during the
quarter. That gain landed in the same net income figure as money made from selling games and
merchandise, even though it came from an entirely different source.

**Why it matters:** worth separating, whenever a company's profit changes a lot more than its sales
did: how much of that change came from running the store, and how much came from something the
company happens to own?
