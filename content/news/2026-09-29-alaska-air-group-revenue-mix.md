### Alaska Air Group is earning more of its money outside the main cabin

53%. That's the share of revenue Alaska Air Group, which runs Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines, says now comes from outside the main cabin. The company said that share rose five points in two years.

It counts premium seats, international flying, its Atmos Rewards loyalty program and cargo in that group. At its 2026 Investor Day it announced a new Premium Reserve cabin and new lounges, and said it aims to push the share to 60% over time.

**Concept:** segment mix

One company can hold several businesses that grow at different speeds. For an airline, the regular economy seat, the roomier seat up front, the points program and the boxes in the cargo hold each bring in money on their own terms. When the mix shifts, the company can change a lot even if the planes look the same from the gate.

**Why it matters:** Next time you book a flight, count the upgrade and credit card offers before checkout, and ask which part of the airline each one is feeding.
