### Walmart's advertising business grew faster than its stores

Walmart reported second-quarter revenue growth of 5.9%, with Walmart US comparable sales up 2.6% and
global e-commerce up 23%. Walmart Connect, the company's US advertising business, grew 43% excluding
VIZIO.

**Concept:** segment mix

Large companies are several businesses reported together. A single company-wide growth figure averages
parts that are moving at very different speeds.

**Why it matters:** a retailer can earn money from selling things and from selling advertising space to
the brands on its shelves. Those are different businesses inside one company.
