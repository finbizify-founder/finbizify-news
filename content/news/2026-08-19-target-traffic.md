### Target's sales rose mostly because more people shopped there

Target reported second-quarter net sales of $26.5 billion, up 5.3% from a year earlier. Comparable
sales grew 3.8 percent, driven by a 3.6 percent increase in comparable traffic, so most of the
increase came from customer count rather than from spending per customer.

**Concept:** traffic vs. ticket

Sales at a store are traffic multiplied by ticket: how many people came in, times how much each one
spent. When those two figures are close together, the growth came from the first one.

**Why it matters:** companies can grow sales by charging more or by attracting more people, and the
second is generally considered the harder one to pull off.
