### Cato is closing more stores as their leases run out

About 120 stores. That's how many Cato now plans to close this fiscal year, after adding roughly 70 more to the list.

Those added stores are all at the end of their leases, so Cato says it won't pay rent on them past 2026.

In past years, it renewed weak stores for one more year to give them time. This year, citing pressure on its customers' spending money, it didn't expect them to improve enough.

**Concept:** fixed vs. variable cost

Some costs move with sales. Sell fewer shirts and you buy fewer shirts. Rent doesn't move. A store owes the same amount whether the parking lot is full or empty, for as long as the lease runs. That's why the end of a lease is usually when a chain decides whether a weak store stays open.

**Why it matters:** When a store near you closes, check whether its lease just ran out. That tends to be when chains make the call.
