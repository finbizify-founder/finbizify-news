### Lululemon's gross margin rose while its sales fell

Lululemon's revenue fell 4% to $2.4 billion in its second quarter. Its gross margin went the other way, up to 60.5%.

But $134.5 million in tariff refunds did much of that lifting. By the company's own figures, the margin would have shrunk without them.

**Concept:** gross margin

Gross margin is the share of each sale left after paying for the thing that was sold. On a pair of leggings, it's the price you pay minus what the leggings cost the company. It can move for reasons separate from how much a company sold, like a one-off refund that lowers its costs for a quarter.

**Why it matters:** When a company's margin rises in a quarter its sales fell, it's fair to ask: did the business change, or did a one-time payment show up?
