### Dick's own stores grew while its newly acquired Foot Locker stores shrank

Dick's Sporting Goods reported that its original business, the DICK'S stores, grew comparable sales
4.9% in the second quarter. Foot Locker, which Dick's has acquired, saw its comparable sales decline
3.6% over the same quarter, which the company said was driven by challenging conditions in the
athletic footwear market.

**Concept:** segment mix

A company that has bought another company reports them together, but the two can be moving in
opposite directions underneath that combined number. One part of the business doing well does not
mean every part is.

**Why it matters:** worth asking of any company built partly through acquisitions: is the part it
built performing differently from the part it bought?
