### Royal Caribbean is buying half of Sandals Resorts

About $3 billion. That's what Royal Caribbean Group agreed on September 23 to pay for a 50% stake in Sandals and Beaches Resorts, the all-inclusive Caribbean resort brands.

So the company behind Royal Caribbean, Celebrity Cruises and Silversea is buying into vacations on land. Sandals is adults-only. Beaches is for families. The deal is expected to close in early 2027.

**Concept:** buying growth vs. building it

A company can grow by doing more of what it already does, like adding ships, or by buying into a business someone else already built. Building can take years, and the company keeps full control. Buying gets there faster, and with a half stake the company shares the steering with the other owner.

**Why it matters:** Next time you see an ad for a cruise or a resort, is that company growing the thing it already does, or paying to own a piece of something new?
