### Spotify raised the ceiling on how much of its own stock it can buy back

Spotify said its board of directors approved an increase of $1.5 billion to the company's share
repurchase program. With $723 million left under the existing program, the increase brings the total
authorization to approximately $2.223 billion.

**Concept:** dividends and buybacks

A company that has more cash than it plans to spend on the business has two common ways to hand it
back to shareholders. It can pay a dividend, which sends cash directly to each holder, or it can buy
back its own shares, which reduces the number of shares that exist so each remaining one covers a
slightly larger slice of the company.

**Why it matters:** an authorization is permission to spend up to an amount rather than a purchase,
and the release says the program doesn't obligate the company to acquire any particular amount of
shares. Worth asking of any buyback announcement: how much of the authorized amount actually gets
spent?
