### Netflix raised prices, and fewer US members left

$12.9 billion. That's what Netflix brought in during its third quarter, up 12% from a year earlier. The company said the growth came mainly from more members and higher prices, plus advertising.

And after this year's price changes, US churn (the share of members who cancel) improved from a year before.

**Concept:** Pricing power

Pricing power is whether a company can charge more without losing a lot of customers. If prices go up and people cancel, the company just swapped customers for dollars. If prices go up and people stay, it has something they'd rather keep paying for. When your subscription costs more and you keep it anyway, you're the one showing that pricing power.

**Why it matters:** Next time a subscription you use gets more expensive, notice what you actually do about it. That choice, multiplied by millions of people, is what pricing power measures.
