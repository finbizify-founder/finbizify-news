### Abercrombie & Fitch's two brands grew at different speeds in its record quarter

Abercrombie & Fitch Co., which runs both the Abercrombie and Hollister brands, reported record
second-quarter net sales of $1.3 billion, up 5% from a year earlier. Abercrombie brand sales grew 8%
while Hollister sales grew 2%.

**Concept:** segment mix

One parent company can own more than one brand a shopper would never connect to each other. Adding
their sales together into one company-wide number hides how differently each one is doing.

**Why it matters:** worth asking of any company that owns multiple brands: is the growth coming from
all of them, or mostly from one?
