# Concepts

Every news item carries exactly one concept tag. Use the slug in frontmatter. **Do not invent a
concept that is not on this list.** If nothing fits, the item is probably not worth publishing.

These twelve were derived from what actually recurred across a real week of consumer business news,
not from a curriculum outline. Add to the list when a genuine gap shows up twice.

When Things to Know is populated in Supabase, these slugs map to it. Until then this file is the
authority.

| Slug | Name | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `comparable-sales` | Comparable sales | Same-store sales. Counting only locations open at least a year, so the existing business can be seen on its own |
| `traffic-vs-ticket` | Traffic vs. ticket | Sales are how many people came in times how much each spent. Which one moved |
| `gross-margin` | Gross margin | What is left from a sale after the cost of the thing sold |
| `segment-mix` | Segment mix | A large company is several businesses reported together, moving at different speeds |
| `pricing-power` | Pricing power | Whether a company can raise prices without losing customers |
| `market-share` | Market share | One company's slice of everything sold in its category |
| `fixed-vs-variable-cost` | Fixed vs. variable cost | Costs that stay the same regardless of volume, against costs that scale with it |
| `inventory` | Inventory | Goods bought or made and not yet sold, and what happens when there is too much or too little |
| `discretionary-vs-staple` | Discretionary vs. staple spending | Purchases that happen on a schedule against purchases that can wait |
| `buying-vs-building-growth` | Buying growth vs. building it | Growing by selling more through the existing business, or by purchasing another one |
| `dividends-and-buybacks` | Dividends and buybacks | Two ways a company returns money to shareholders |
| `one-time-vs-ongoing` | One-time items vs. ongoing business | Separating money from running the business from money from a separate event |
